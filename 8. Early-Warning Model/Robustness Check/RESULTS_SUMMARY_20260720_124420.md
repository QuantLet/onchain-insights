# Threshold Robustness Check

- Run ID: `20260720_124420`
- Thresholds: 10, 15, 20, and 25 bps
- Fixed settings: 24-hour target window, both depeg sides, robust scaler, 5-fold expanding-window CV
- Grid: 6 alpha values x 4 models x 4 thresholds = 96 CV model-alpha results
- Full retraining: 12 model-alpha candidates selected by the same top-2 CV AUC and top-2 CV AUPRC rule

## Interpretation

- For 10-20 bps, Random Forest is the most stable AUC performer. This supports robustness of the broad model-ranking conclusion, but not of the exact best alpha or metric level.
- AUPRC changes sharply with event prevalence, so AUPRC values should not be compared across thresholds without also checking positive rates and lift.
- The 20 bps result is already based on only 1-3 target episodes per CV test fold. It is useful as a stricter robustness case, but should be interpreted cautiously.
- The 25 bps result is a stress test only. Every CV test fold contains the same 24 positive hours from one target episode, and the XGBoost full-retraining validation step reported a single-class validation set. Its high AUC and lift are not reliable evidence of generalisation.
- The full-retraining test period overlaps the CV test periods. Those metrics are diagnostic rather than an independent final holdout.

Primary robustness results should therefore emphasise 10, 15, and 20 bps, with 25 bps reported separately as an extreme-threshold stress test.
